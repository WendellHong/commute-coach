How the app works

1
Claude, in your project chat
Writes the session: spoken script, answer windows, and the MP3 audio with caption timings.
2
You, once per batch, 2 minutes
Upload Claude's files (DayNN.json plus its three MP3s) to the Drive folder "Commute Coach audio". Then tap "Load new sessions from Drive" above.
3
This page
Downloads the audio once and keeps it on your device, so later plays start fast.
4
Your commute
Pick a session, then Play all 3 parts or one part, while parked. Captions follow the audio. At each chime the microphone opens; stop talking for a few seconds and it moves on. Pause keeps your place: you can switch to another part and come back.
5
Saved automatically
Each answer goes to the Google Sheet (transcript, length) and your recording to Drive. Every part you play is logged in the Usage tab: when, how long, from where to where. Review, Practice shows it as charts.
6
Evening, Sync tab
Build the text of your answers, copy it, paste it into the Claude chat.
7
Claude
Replies with feedback and the next session. Import it, and the cycle repeats. Feedback shows in the Review tab.

Where things live

Google Sheet "Commute Coach data": Content, Responses, Feedback, Log, Usage, Audio, Versions. Drive folder "Commute Coach audio": lesson MP3s and your answer recordings. This page: only your settings and a cached copy of the audio.

If the microphone does not ask for permission

The page must be opened from its own https address (GitHub Pages or similar), not inside Google's Apps Script page or another app's embedded view. On iPhone use Safari, tap the aA icon, Website Settings, Microphone, Allow. On a PC click the lock icon next to the address, allow Microphone, and reload.
