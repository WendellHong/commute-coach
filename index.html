How the app works

1
Claude, in your project chat
Writes the session: spoken script, answer windows, and the MP3 audio with caption timings.
2
You, once per session, 2 minutes
Paste Claude's JSON into Sync, Import. Upload the MP3 files to the Drive folder "Commute Coach audio" and run registerAudio in Apps Script.
3
This page
Downloads the audio once and keeps it on your device, so later plays start fast.
4
Your commute
Tap Start while parked. The coach and interviewer speak; captions follow. At each chime the microphone opens; stop talking for a few seconds and it moves on.
5
Saved automatically
Each answer goes to the Google Sheet (transcript, length) and your recording to Drive.
6
Evening, Sync tab
Build the text of your answers, copy it, paste it into the Claude chat.
7
Claude
Replies with feedback and the next session. Import it, and the cycle repeats. Feedback shows in the Review tab.

Where things live

Google Sheet "Commute Coach data": Content, Responses, Feedback, Log, Audio, Versions. Drive folder "Commute Coach audio": lesson MP3s and your answer recordings. This page: only your settings and a cached copy of the audio.

If the microphone does not ask for permission

The page must be opened from its own https address (GitHub Pages or similar), not inside Google's Apps Script page or another app's embedded view. On iPhone use Safari, tap the aA icon, Website Settings, Microphone, Allow. On a PC click the lock icon next to the address, allow Microphone, and reload.
