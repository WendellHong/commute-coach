1. Send my recordings to Claude (best feedback)

Makes one zip file with your recorded answers and their prompts. Download it and attach it in the Claude chat. Claude transcribes the audio accurately and reviews content, length, pace and pauses.

1b. Or copy the text version

Paste the result into the Claude chat. Claude replies with feedback and tomorrow's session.

2. Load what Claude sent back

Best: save Claude's reply file (Claude-reply_....json) into Drive, folder Commute Coach audio > Claude replies. Then tap Load. Each file moves to "Imported" once loaded, so nothing is loaded twice; transcripts, feedback, scores and reports carry ids, so even a repeat updates instead of adding a copy.

3. Prep book (Google Doc + PDF)

Collects your book entries (track record, stories, company notes, resume lines), your latest answers with Claude's suggested wording, and your progress. You get an editable Google Doc and a PDF copy in your Drive folder.

4. Clean up duplicates

Removes answers that were saved twice, repeated notes and reports, repeated script and practice-log lines, and moves older copies of session files in Drive to a folder called "Old copies". Your recordings are never deleted.

Optional: automatic feedback

Needs your own API key in the Apps Script properties; it costs API usage.

Every day

1
Drive
The Drive tab shows the next drive with its three parts. Tap "Start drive", or one part by its name; other days are under "All sessions". The screen turns dark with three large buttons: back 15 seconds, Pause, and forward (it becomes Done while you answer). At each chime, answer out loud: a large countdown shows the seconds left, and a beep sounds 5 seconds before the end.
2
After the drive - Sync
Tap "Pack new answers", download the zip, and attach it in the Claude chat.
3
Claude
Sends back one Claude-reply file per day: an accurate transcript of every answer, a scored note with a better wording, and the day's report.
4
You - Drive, then Review
Save the reply files into Drive, folder "Commute Coach audio > Claude replies". Open Review and tap "Load now". A progress bar shows each file; loaded files move to "Imported".
5
Review - Sessions
Open the day's page: the script, your answers in every version, sample answers, Claude's notes, and the follow-ups to tick off. Tap "Practice again" on any answer you want to improve; the new version goes into your next pack.

If something goes wrong

The microphone does not ask for permission

The page must be opened from its own https address (GitHub Pages), not inside another app. On iPhone use Safari: tap the aA icon, Website Settings, Microphone, Allow. On a PC click the lock icon next to the address, allow Microphone, and reload.

"Nothing was recorded for that answer"

The phone cut the microphone off, usually because the screen locked or another app took the audio. The app reopens the microphone for the next answer. Keep the screen on while driving, and use "Practice again" for the lost answer.

The countdown ends early

With "Move on when I stop talking" on, 3 seconds of silence after you have spoken ends the window; the screen says "Silence: moving on". Turn it off under Options if you want the full time every time.

A new session does not appear

Check that DayNN.json and its three MP3s are in the Drive folder itself, not in a subfolder, then tap "Load new sessions from Drive" under Connect.

Review looks out of date, or shows something twice

Tap "Load now" if a reply is waiting. For repeats, use Sync, "Clean up duplicates".

Where things live

Google Sheet "Commute Coach data": Content (scripts), Meta (titles and introductions), Responses (your answers), Feedback, Reports, Followups, Book, Usage, Audio, Log, Versions. Drive folder "Commute Coach audio": session files and MP3s, your answer recordings, answer packs; inside it "Claude replies" (drop reply files here), "Claude replies > Imported" (already loaded) and "Old copies". This page keeps only your settings and a cached copy of the audio.

Updating the app

Backend: in Apps Script, replace the code with the new Code.gs, save, run setup() once, then Deploy, Manage deployments, Edit, New version, Deploy. Page: replace index.html on GitHub. Versions shows whether the two match.
